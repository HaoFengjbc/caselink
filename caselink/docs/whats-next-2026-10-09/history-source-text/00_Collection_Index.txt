Journalism and Online Speech: Case Follow-Up Collection
INDEPENDENT RESEARCH / OCTOBER 2026
COLLECTION GUIDE
This collection contains 21 independent English updates: 20 case reports and one PECA legal-reform
report. The 24 supplied PDFs include three duplicate copies. Coverage ends on 9 October 2026;
individual reports identify their last verified event and any unresolved outcome. The reports are
independent research, not CFJ or TrialWatch publications.
HOW TO READ THE STATUS
Quashing and acquittal are judicial outcomes. A pardon or release does not by itself establish that a
conviction was erased. International findings and registered complaints are separated from domestic
implementation. An unknown appellate result is stated as unknown. Later separate prosecutions and
professional updates are identified in each report.
ONLINE SPEECH
01. Thailand v. Wuth Boonlert and Samak Donnapee
Acquittal upheld on appeal; later separate defamation case involving Wuth settled
Dated update covered: 26 September 2023
The latest verified decision in the original Chaiwat prosecution is the Court of Appeal's 22 December 2020 acquittal. The 26
September 2023 event concerns a distinct case involving Wuth and another defendant, not Samak. No Supreme Court
disposition or certificate of finality in the original proceedings was verified.
02. Kazakhstan v. Alnur Ilyashev
Original restrictions reportedly completed in part; UN remedy outstanding; separate 2026 investigation
Dated update covered: 4 September 2026
Freedom Now explicitly reports release from house arrest on 17 April 2023. A 30 April 2025 interview says the five-year
public-activity ban was due to expire later that year, not that expiry had already been formally confirmed. September 2026
reporting describes release after questioning in a separate fraud investigation as a witness with a right to defence and an
undertaking to appear. No current custodial order or domestic reversal of the 2020 conviction was verified.
03. Russian Federation v. Alexander Pichugin
ECtHR found freedom-of-expression violation; domestic reversal and payment unverified
Dated update covered: 13 November 2025
The ECtHR's final committee judgment addresses the original speech conviction and a separate protest case. The EUR
10,000 award covers the applicant's combined findings, not exclusively the speech prosecution. A foreign-agent designation
on 7 November 2025 is separately reported. No new prison sentence, domestic acquittal, or payment of the Strasbourg
award was verified.
04. Section 20 of Pakistan's PECA
2022 partial invalidation; 2025 statutory expansion; enforcement verified in 2026
Dated update covered: 16 September 2026
Latest located court event is Bilal Ghauri's conditional post-arrest bail, reported on 17 September 2026. The 2022 ruling
pre-dates and is discussed in the original. It does not establish repeal of all Section 20. No final merits disposition of the 2025
constitutional challenges or definitive appellate treatment of the 2022 ruling was verified.
Research cutoff: 9 October 2026

05. Tunisia v. Emna Chargui
Conviction appealed; subsequent appellate disposition unverified
Dated update covered: 13 July 2020
The dated conviction is taken from the original report. The original also confirms an appeal and asylum in Germany by June
2021, without precise event dates. No later case-disposing order was verified through 9 October 2026. Amnesty's 15 July
2020 article describes sentencing 'yesterday', conflicting with the original's 13 July date.
CRIMINALIZING REPORTING
06. Bangladesh v. Shahidul Alam
High Court quashed case on 7 August 2025; subsequent appellate position unverified
Dated update covered: 7 August 2025
Quashing is corroborated by local court reporting and Amnesty. No later State appeal, leave application, stay or appellate
disposition was verified. The 2022 appellate proceedings concerned an earlier challenge and must not be mistaken for an
appeal from the 2025 order.
07. Belarus v. Katsiaryna Andreyeva and Daria Chultsova
Chultsova released in 2022; Andreyeva pardoned and released in March 2026
Dated update covered: 24 March 2026
Latest dated follow-up is PEN International's 24 March response to the 19 March release. Chultsova's September 2022
release followed completion of her term; Andreyeva's 2026 release followed a pardon after a separate treason conviction.
Neither event establishes that either original conviction was quashed.
08. Georgia v. Mzia Amaglobeli
Reported imprisoned; Supreme Court declined cassation; ECHR complaints filed
Dated update covered: 21 September 2026
21 September is the publication date of a CMIS report covering September 2025-August 2026, not a new detention order.
Latest verified domestic criminal disposition: Supreme Court nonadmission on 4 March 2026. A conviction-related ECHR
application was reportedly filed 26 June and registered 3 July. No release or ECHR merits judgment was verified through 9
October.
09. Guatemala v. Jose Ruben Zamora
Under house arrest; renewed annulment and retrial order; foreign travel refused
Dated update covered: 8 October 2026
National Press Club's 8 October 2026 statement explicitly confirms ongoing house arrest. Latest substantive judicial
development located is the 13 August refusal to lift his exit restriction. July finality applies to the particular withdrawn appeal
over substitute measures, not to the criminal prosecution or retrial.
10. Hong Kong SAR v. Bao Choy Yuk Ling
Both convictions quashed by Court of Final Appeal in June 2023
Dated update covered: 6 March 2026
Latest related judicial event is a separate March 2026 judicial review of the revised vehicle-records policy. It did not reinstate
Choy's convictions. Her criminal appeal was allowed on 5 June 2023 in FACC 2/2023, [2023] HKCFA 12.
Research cutoff: 9 October 2026

11. Kazakhstan v. Aigul Utepova
UN individual communication registered; last confirmed pending in 2025; present restrictions unverified
Dated update covered: 2025
Latest verified procedural status appears in TrialWatch's submission for the June-July 2025 Human Rights Committee
session: Aigul Utepova v. Kazakhstan, No. 4903/2023, pending. Exact submission day is unstated. This does not establish a
UN merits decision, domestic reversal, release, termination of activity restrictions, or removal of banking restrictions as of
October 2026.
12. Malaysia v. Malaysiakini and Steven Gan
Company's contempt conviction left in place after review dismissal; Gan acquitted
Dated update covered: 29 March 2022
The latest verified case-disposing event is rejection of the company's review application, not a new conviction of Gan.
Payment of the original fine was reported. No subsequent reversal was verified; this does not independently establish
exhaustion of every conceivable remedy.
13. Belarus v. Marina Zolotova
Pardoned and released in December 2025; living in exile in 2026
Dated update covered: 16 September 2026
Latest related event is the release of later TUT.BY co-defendant Liudmila Chekina on 16 September 2026, not a new
disposition for Zolotova. Zolotova's latest substantive personal account located is dated 7 August 2026. Her pardon and
release do not establish that either conviction was overturned.
14. Morocco v. Hajar Raissouni et al.
Pardoned in 2019; no subsequent acquittal or vacatur verified
Dated update covered: 2 November 2022
The latest located subject-specific follow-up is a published interview, not a new judicial disposition. The pardon and release
occurred on 16 October 2019 and were already recorded in the original report. The record located through 9 October 2026
does not establish that the convictions were subsequently quashed.
15. Morocco v. Omar Radi II
Cassation rejected in 2023; pardoned and released in 2024, vacatur unverified
Dated update covered: 6 October 2026
The latest dated follow-up is an interview publication. The last verified change to custody is pardon and release on 29 July
2024. PEN's March 2025 profile still reported uncertainty over quashing. The pardon is not described here as an acquittal,
and the disposition of civil compensation is unverified.
16. Morocco v. Omar Radi I
2020 suspended sentence; appeal filed, later disposition not verified
Dated update covered: 1 October 2026
The 1 October 2026 event is a new Amnesty investigation concerning Radi, not a ruling in the tweet prosecution. The last
verified proceeding in this particular case is the 17 March 2020 conviction and notice of appeal recorded in the original.
Radi's July 2024 pardon and release in the separate later case cannot be assumed to have cleared this conviction.

17. Russian Federation v. Svetlana Prokopyeva
ECtHR freedom-of-expression victory; later separate foreign-agent fine under appeal
Dated update covered: 15 May 2026
A final ECtHR committee judgment on 22 January 2026 found an Article 10 violation concerning the original conviction and
awarded EUR 7,500. Domestic reversal and payment were not verified. The latest verified separate proceeding is a 15 May
2026 report confirming receipt of an appeal against a 30,000-ruble foreign-agent-label fine; an 8 June hearing was
scheduled, but its outcome was not verified. Historical terrorism-list removal and foreign-agent designation are different
events.
18. Turkey v. Cansu Piskin
Ten-month sentence with five-year deferred pronouncement; closure unverified
Dated update covered: 7 May 2019
This date is the last verified event in Piskin's own prosecution, not the latest appearance of her name online. Later reporting
identifies her as an observer or author in other journalists' cases. No objection decision, activation of sentence, dismissal
after supervision, acquittal, or international ruling in her own case was verified through 9 October 2026.
19. Uganda v. Moses Bwayo
Charge dismissed in August 2020; no reinstatement verified
Dated update covered: 10 December 2025
The latest verified follow-up is an official film-program announcement crediting Bwayo, not a court outcome. Dismissal for
want of prosecution on 10 August 2020 was already in the original. His April 2024 first-person account places his move to the
United States in March 2022 and describes seeking asylum; a grant was not verified. A same-name Ugandan civil case is an
unconfirmed lead, not an attributed lawsuit or judgment.
CITIZEN JOURNALISM
20. Malaysia v. Dian Abdullah
Guilty plea and RM 10,000 fine reported in December 2021
Dated update covered: 21 December 2021
Personal-case event: sentencing on 21 December 2021, following a guilty plea on 6 December. Fine payment, default
imprisonment and later appeal were not verified. Section 233 developments through 6 February 2026 concern separate
constitutional litigation and do not establish reversal of Dian's conviction.
21. Tunisia v. Myriam Bribri
December 2021 conviction appealed; appellate result unverified
Dated update covered: 9 October 2022
Latest dated follow-up is an NGO statement announcing an expected 12 October 2022 ruling, not verification that the ruling
occurred. Sources disagree on the December conviction and appeal dates. Amnesty describes a suspended custodial
sentence; earlier sources describe provisional liberty. No current custody status is established.
Research cutoff: 9 October 2026
