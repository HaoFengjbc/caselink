BACKGROUND AND PROCEDURAL HISTORY
The prosecution concerned a Facebook post in May 2020 reproducing a satirical public-health text in the
form of a Quranic verse. Chargui was prosecuted under Articles 52 and 53 of Decree 115 of 2011,
concerning incitement of religious hatred and interference with authorized religious observance. The
report distinguished an irreverent expression from evidence of incitement to violence or a demonstrable
threat to others. 1
TrialWatch identified restrictions on access to counsel during questioning, shortcomings in the
prosecution's presentation of evidence, and concerns about legality, freedom of expression and the
presumption of innocence. It reported that counsel appealed within the statutory period, but that her
absence from Tunisia complicated participation in the appeal. Those are historical findings and procedural
descriptions from June 2021, not independently established descriptions of a proceeding still pending in
2026. 1

SUBSEQUENT DEVELOPMENTS
Amnesty's contemporary statement reported that Chargui was at liberty and intended to appeal in July
2020. It urged reversal of the conviction and investigation of threats against her. These are a dated
custody observation and an NGO's advocacy position, not proof of subsequent release, appellate success
or current protection. Its relative sentencing date differs from the original, and a certified judgment would
be needed to reconcile that discrepancy. 2
CFJ's 25 June 2021 announcement corroborates the publication of its critical TrialWatch assessment. The
report page dated 26 January 2022 repeats the report's subject matter; neither source supplies an
appellate disposition. They therefore cannot move the verified procedural history forward merely because
their publication dates are later. 3 4
