EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
LATEST VERIFIED POSITION
2020 suspended sentence; appeal filed, later disposition not verified
The 1 October 2026 event is a new Amnesty investigation concerning Radi, not a ruling in the tweet prosecution.
The last verified proceeding in this particular case is the 17 March 2020 conviction and notice of appeal recorded
in the original. Radi's July 2024 pardon and release in the separate later case cannot be assumed to have
cleared this conviction.
Omar Radi's first TrialWatch report concerns punishment for a tweet criticizing a judge, not the later
espionage and sexual-assault prosecution. The court imposed four months' suspended imprisonment and
a 500-dirham fine on 17 March 2020; the original records that lawyers filed notice of appeal that day. No
subsequent disposition of that appeal was verified. 1
Radi was pardoned and released on 29 July 2024 in the later detention context. The latest located
substantive investigation concerning him was published by Amnesty on 1 October 2026. Neither
establishes that the tweet judgment was reversed or that its fine and record were removed. 4 7

BACKGROUND AND PROCEDURAL HISTORY
Radi's April 2019 tweet criticized judge Lahcen Talfi after lengthy sentences against Hirak Rif protest
leaders were upheld. Radi was questioned on 18 April, detained on 26 December, and granted bail on 31
December 2019. The merits hearing occurred on 5 March 2020, followed by conviction under Penal Code
Article 263 for insulting the judiciary. The original identifies case number 2103/14047/2019 and a defense
declaration of appeal filed on 17 March 2020. That is evidence of an actual appeal step, not simply a
proposal to appeal. 1
TrialWatch expert Professor Hannah Garry awarded the proceedings a D grade. The report criticized
restrictions on expression, the treatment of intent, judicial impartiality, and the failure to give meaningful
consideration to the defense's Press Code arguments. Its concerns related to the criminalization of
criticism of judicial conduct; they are not a determination about the separate allegations subsequently
brought against Radi. 1

SUBSEQUENT DEVELOPMENTS
CFJ's September 2020 statement urged an appellate reversal while acknowledging the separate new
prosecution. Amnesty's April 2021 publication likewise treated the earlier suspended sentence as distinct.
These sources reinforce case identification but do not answer how the tweet appeal ended. 2 3
After the 2024 release, CPJ reported threatening telephone calls to Radi and uncertainty over whether
pardons removed convictions. Amnesty's May 2025 educational account described a criminal record
impeding his work in Morocco. These are relevant personal consequences, but neither source assigns the
obstacle specifically to the tweet judgment. 5 6
Amnesty's October 2026 publication adds surveillance allegations concerning Radi, not a tweet-case
judgment. Its evidence is an NGO assessment, not a judicial disposition. 7

CONCLUSION AND OUTSTANDING RECORDS
The accurate case-specific update is an unverified appeal outcome following the 2020 conviction, with
separate later release and continuing reported consequences for Radi. Retain the original D assessment
as attributed history; do not present the 2024 pardon as an acquittal in this case. 1 4
RECORDS NOT VERIFIED
1. What decision, if any, followed the 17 March 2020 notice of appeal in case 2103/14047/2019? Obtain
the appeal docket and judgment.
2. Did the July 2024 pardon expressly include the tweet conviction, or only the later sentence? The
operative individualized instrument was not obtained.
3. Was the 500-dirham fine paid, remitted, or cancelled, and does this conviction remain on Radi's criminal
record?
4. Did a constitutional or international proceeding specifically concerning the tweet judgment produce a
decision? No such outcome was verified.
Failure to locate an update leaves a gap in the available evidence; it does not establish that no later proceeding or change
occurred. Any present-status claim should be read with the dated scope stated in the executive summary.
